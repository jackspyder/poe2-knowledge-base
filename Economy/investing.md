# POE2 League Start Investing (Wealth Scaling)

Expert strategies for early-league capital growth in v0.4+.

## 1. THE DIVINE/CHAOS "HEDGE"
- **Timing**: Days 1-4.
- **Strategy**: Convert all raw **Exalted Orbs** into **Chaos Orbs** and **Divine Orbs** immediately. 
- **Reasoning**: Exalts are the base drop in T15+ maps. Holding them causes you to lose purchasing power as the exchange rate for Chaos (currently ~7.4:1) and Divines (currently **~380:1**) climbs rapidly.

## 2. BULK FRAGMENT ACCUMULATION
- **Targets**: **Simulacrum/Ritual Splinters** and **Logbooks**.
- **Strategy**: Buy individual items from low-tier traders using Exalted Orbs.
- **Payoff**: Resell in bulk for Divine Orbs only. Experts pay a 20%+ premium for Divine-priced bulk lots.

## 3. META-UNIQUE SNIPING
- **Week 1 Meta**: Track the Druid/Ranger ladder share.
- **Investment**: Use early Exalt capital to buy mid-roll versions of mandatory uniques while they are priced in "low numbers."
- **Payoff**: Resell for multiple Divines in Week 2.

## 4. BULK MATERIAL FLIPPING
- **Items**: **Blacksmith's Whetstones**, **Armourer's Scraps**, **Gemcutter's Prisms**.
- **Strategy**: Buy for Exalted Orbs in the first 72 hours. These are consistently undervalued early.
- **Payoff**: Resell for Chaos/Divines once the "Second Wave" of crafters begins scaling gear.
