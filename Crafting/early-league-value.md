# POE2 Early League Value Crafting (Days 1-3)

High-velocity crafts for generating early capital. Focus on item level (ilvl) 81-82 bases from early Red Maps.

## 1. SPELLCASTER WEAPONS (+1 GEMS)
- **Base**: ilvl 82+ Staff or Wand with high base Casting Speed.
- **Process**: 
  1.  Transmute/Augment for **+1 to all Spell Skill Gems** (Prefix).
  2.  Regal for a usable secondary (Casting Speed / Crit Chance).
- **Expert Value**: Sells for 2-5 Divines early to players rushing Red Maps.

## 2. ESSENCE-FORCED ARMOR
- **Process**: Use **Greater Essences of Body** (Tier 3+) on ilvl 81+ Plate or Silk bases.
- **Goal**: Hit high Flat Life (Prefix) + 2x T2 Resistances (Suffix).
- **Strategy**: Do not waste Exalts here. Sell "good enough" gear for 1-2 Divines to fund your own T15 Waystones.

## 3. MOVEMENT SPEED BOOTS
- **Base**: Any ilvl 80+ base with high implicit speed or armor.
- **Process**: Essence spam for **30%+ Movement Speed** (Prefix).
- **Expert Tip**: Expert players will pay a premium for boots that solve both Speed and a single high Resistance (40%+).

## 4. UNCUT GEM FLIPPING
- **Source**: Farm **Uncut Support Gems** from early Delirium/Ritual.
- **Process**: Check the Day 1-2 meta (e.g., Druid or Ranger). Use your Uncut Gems to create the most popular support gems.
- **Liquidity**: S-Tier. Sells for 1:1 Exalted Orb ratios in the first 48 hours.
