# PoE2 0.5.0 Minions, Companions, Totems, and Allies

Source: [Content Update 0.5.0 captured patch notes](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md)

## Companions and Ally Presence

- Spirit Walker is a new Huntress Ascendancy centered on spirits, beast subduing, and spectral companions. [L288](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L288)
- 19 Companion-themed passive skills were added. [L398](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L398)
- Trusted Kinship now gives more reservation efficiency for Companion Skills and less reservation efficiency for non-Companion Skills, removing its previous defence exchange. [L406](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L406)
- Tame Beast summons newly tamed beasts immediately if you have enough Spirit, gives summoned beasts much more damage, and moved down to minimum gem level 7. [L511](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L511), [L953](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L953)
- Remnant-sharing with allies no longer triggers stats worded as "when you collect a Remnant." [L282](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L282)
- Modifiers that apply to allies in your Presence no longer stack in parties when multiple players provide modifiers of the same type, except for Leer Cast's allies-in-presence damage modifier. [L277](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L277)
- Hollow Mask shares Remnant effects with allies in your presence and improves Remnant Skill reservation efficiency. [L569](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L569)

## Minion Skills and Supports

- Minions removed due to resource changes now prioritize younger minions, reducing wasted cooldown abilities. [L280](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L280)
- Minions under Last Gasp or Tecrod's Revenge, and certain totems under Heartwood Oracle, cannot die from further damage during the effect. [L281](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L281)
- Minion Splash and Minion Splash II Strength Support Gems were added. [L355](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L355)
- Commanding Rage now grants 2% increased Minion Attack Speed per 5 Rage instead of 1% per Rage. [L417](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L417)
- Infernal Legion I and II now use 10% of minion maximum Life for self-damage and ignite base damage, down from 20%; Infernal Legion II also grants Fire Resistance. Infernal Legion III can no longer be obtained. [L534-L536](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L534-L536)
- Living Lightning no longer replaces minions when already at maximum minion count. [L537](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L537)
- Rage I, II, and III can now support Minion Skills. [L544](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L544)
- Chober Chaber now grants +2-3 to all Minion Skills. [L563](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L563)
- Genesis Tree can craft new Caster and Minion mods onto rings and belts. [L143](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L143)
- Quipolatl's Thesis now starts Energy Shield Recharge when Minions are Reformed if socketed into gloves. [L963](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L963)
- The Bogfelled Commoner now has a Spectre variant for the boss version of the monster. [L976](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L976)

## Totems, Banners, and Bells

- Ancestral Bond makes totem placement costless and charge-free, keeps 75 Spirit reservation per totem, and doubles totem limit. [L405](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L405)
- Ancestral Warrior Totem loses a hidden delay and describes delay as half its attack time. [L472](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L472)
- Spell Totem can be used while moving and received an updated animation. [L509](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L509)
- Defiance Banner, Dread Banner, and War Banner lose movement penalty and gain a 6 metre base aura radius. [L478](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L478)
- Small Banner Area passives and Banner Area Tactician values are reduced. [L461-L462](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L461-L462)
- Refraction I and II cause supported Banner skills to grant allies Deflection Rating based on Evasion Rating. [L546-L547](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L546-L547)
- Soul Mantle loses reduced Totem Life and gains +75 Spirit. [L582](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L582)
- Tempest Bell can now be Ancestrally Boosted as a Strike, has a maximum of 3 active Bells, and has adjusted shockwave damage and trigger frequency. [L512](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L512)

## Idols and Presence Scaling

- New Idols drop from monsters possessed by Azmerian Spirits. [L354](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L354)
- All Idols can socket into Sceptres, creating many allies-in-presence and companion-in-presence hooks. [L638](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L638)
- Idol of Eeshta, Grold, Ralakesh, Sirrius, Thruldana, Boar, Owl, Ox, Primate, Snake, and Wolf all add ally, companion, minion, or presence-facing effects. [L640-L656](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L640-L656)
- Azmeri Spirits released from monsters retain power and empower other spirits from the monster. [L684](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L684)
- Empowered Minions now update their displayed "Minion" text. [L705](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L705)

## Mirage Archer and Mirage Deadeye

- Mirage Archer and Mirage Deadeye can now be used with channelled skills, including Snipe, Plasma Blast, and Detonating Arrow. [L494](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L494)
- Bug fixes improve Mirage Archer/Deadeye target rotation, prevent invalid skill socketing, and address unintended supportability. [L744-L745](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L744-L745), [L793](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L793)

## Bug Fixes

- Minion Instability now bypasses Last Gasp correctly. [L747](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L747)
- Supports restricted to "Skills you use yourself" no longer incorrectly apply to clones such as Mirage Archer or Feral Invocation, or to minions you summon. [L756](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L756)
- Several Unearth supportability bugs were fixed, and Bone Constructs from Unearth no longer stand still when summoned by a totem. [L757](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L757), [L760](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L760)
- Fire Skills used by Spell Totems or by Visages from Align Fate no longer generate Raging Spirits. [L762-L763](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L762-L763)
- Tactician's Cannons, Ready! now causes totems to attack when using the listed player skills. [L777](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L777)
- Player-created Azmeri Spirit Possession modifiers now use skills and function correctly, dealing more damage and using skills more often. [L786](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L786)
- Shockwave Totem can no longer be socketed into Ancestral Warrior Totem. [L790](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L790)
- Infernal Legion no longer always crits whenever it has any critical hit chance. [L794](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L794)
- Reviving minions intended to inherit player movement speed now do so, including Smith of Kitava's Manifest Weapon, dismounted Rhoa Mount, and Hysseg's Claw Hyenas. [L795](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L795)
- Grim Feast no longer sometimes fails to revive minions. [L799](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L799)
- Boss skills can correctly target player minions. [L811](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L811)
- Essence Monster pack minions now receive Rare-pack stat increases. [L833](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L833)
- Varashta's Blessing now states that it does not modify minion skills. [L850](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L850)
- Totems in the Skills panel no longer incorrectly prompt activation under Ancestral Bond, and presence radius indicators no longer stop working in some cases. [L854](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L854), [L860](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L860)

## Practical Read

- Companion and presence scaling are now major organizational concepts. Sceptre Idol setups likely need their own build notes.
- Totem builds gained clearer Ancestral Bond rules and Spirit-based constraints, while Soul Mantle's change is a meaningful Spirit pivot.
- Minion supportability widened through Rage supports and new Minion Splash supports, but multiple bug fixes indicate old assumptions about triggered, revived, or player-created minions need retesting.
