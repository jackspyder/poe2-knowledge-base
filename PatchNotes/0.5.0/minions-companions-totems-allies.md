# PoE2 0.5.0 Minions, Companions, Totems, and Allies

Source: [Content Update 0.5.0 captured patch notes](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md)

## Companions and Ally Presence

- Spirit Walker is a new Huntress Ascendancy centered on spirits, beast subduing, and spectral companions. [L276](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L276)
- 19 Companion-themed passive skills were added. [L383](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L383)
- Trusted Kinship now gives more reservation efficiency for Companion Skills and less reservation efficiency for non-Companion Skills, removing its previous defence exchange. [L387](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L387)
- Tame Beast summons newly tamed beasts immediately if you have enough Spirit and gives summoned beasts much more damage. [L483](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L483)
- Remnant-sharing with allies no longer triggers stats worded as "when you collect a Remnant." [L270](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L270)
- Hollow Mask shares Remnant effects with allies in your presence and improves Remnant Skill reservation efficiency. [L538](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L538)

## Minion Skills and Supports

- Minions removed due to resource changes now prioritize younger minions, reducing wasted cooldown abilities. [L268](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L268)
- Minions under Last Gasp or Tecrod's Revenge, and certain totems under Heartwood Oracle, cannot die from further damage during the effect. [L269](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L269)
- Minion Splash and Minion Splash II Strength Support Gems were added. [L341](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L341)
- Commanding Rage now grants 2% increased Minion Attack Speed per 5 Rage instead of 1% per Rage. [L397](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L397)
- Infernal Legion I and II now use 10% of minion maximum Life for self-damage and ignite base damage, down from 20%; Infernal Legion II also grants Fire Resistance. Infernal Legion III can no longer be obtained. [L506-L508](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L506-L508)
- Living Lightning no longer replaces minions when already at maximum minion count. [L509](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L509)
- Rage I, II, and III can now support Minion Skills. [L514](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L514)
- Chober Chaber now grants +2-3 to all Minion Skills. [L533](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L533)
- Genesis Tree can craft new Caster and Minion mods onto rings and belts. [L143](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L143)

## Totems, Banners, and Bells

- Ancestral Bond makes totem placement costless and charge-free, keeps 75 Spirit reservation per totem, and doubles totem limit. [L386](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L386)
- Ancestral Warrior Totem loses a hidden delay and describes delay as half its attack time. [L445](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L445)
- Defiance Banner, Dread Banner, and War Banner lose movement penalty and gain a 6 metre base aura radius. [L450](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L450)
- Small Banner Area passives and Banner Area Tactician values are reduced. [L434-L435](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L434-L435)
- Refraction I and II cause supported Banner skills to grant allies Deflection Rating based on Evasion Rating. [L516-L517](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L516-L517)
- Soul Mantle loses reduced Totem Life and gains +75 Spirit. [L551](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L551)
- Tempest Bell can now be Ancestrally Boosted as a Strike, has a maximum of 3 active Bells, and has adjusted shockwave damage and trigger frequency. [L484](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L484)

## Idols and Presence Scaling

- New Idols drop from monsters possessed by Azmerian Spirits. [L340](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L340)
- All Idols can socket into Sceptres, creating many allies-in-presence and companion-in-presence hooks. [L605](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L605)
- Idol of Eeshta, Grold, Ralakesh, Sirrius, Thruldana, Boar, Owl, Ox, Primate, Snake, and Wolf all add ally, companion, minion, or presence-facing effects. [L607-L623](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L607-L623)
- Azmeri Spirits released from monsters retain power and empower other spirits from the monster. [L651](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L651)
- Empowered Minions now update their displayed "Minion" text. [L671](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L671)

## Mirage Archer and Mirage Deadeye

- Mirage Archer and Mirage Deadeye can now be used with channelled skills, including Snipe, Plasma Blast, and Detonating Arrow. [L466](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L466)
- Bug fixes improve Mirage Archer/Deadeye target rotation, prevent invalid skill socketing, and address unintended supportability. [L708-L709](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L708-L709), [L742](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L742)

## Bug Fixes

- Minion Instability now bypasses Last Gasp correctly. [L711](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L711)
- Tactician's Cannons, Ready! now causes totems to attack when using the listed player skills. [L726](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L726)
- Player-created Azmeri Spirit Possession modifiers now use skills and function correctly, dealing more damage and using skills more often. [L735](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L735)
- Shockwave Totem can no longer be socketed into Ancestral Warrior Totem. [L739](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L739)
- Infernal Legion no longer always crits whenever it has any critical hit chance. [L743](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L743)
- Reviving minions intended to inherit player movement speed now do so, including Smith of Kitava's Manifest Weapon, dismounted Rhoa Mount, and Hysseg's Claw Hyenas. [L744](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L744)
- Grim Feast no longer sometimes fails to revive minions. [L748](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L748)
- Boss skills can correctly target player minions. [L759](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L759)
- Essence Monster pack minions now receive Rare-pack stat increases. [L781](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L781)
- Varashta's Blessing now states that it does not modify minion skills. [L798](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L798)
- Totems in the Skills panel no longer incorrectly prompt activation under Ancestral Bond, and presence radius indicators no longer stop working in some cases. [L802](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L802), [L808](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L808)

## Practical Read

- Companion and presence scaling are now major organizational concepts. Sceptre Idol setups likely need their own build notes.
- Totem builds gained clearer Ancestral Bond rules and Spirit-based constraints, while Soul Mantle's change is a meaningful Spirit pivot.
- Minion supportability widened through Rage supports and new Minion Splash supports, but multiple bug fixes indicate old assumptions about triggered, revived, or player-created minions need retesting.
