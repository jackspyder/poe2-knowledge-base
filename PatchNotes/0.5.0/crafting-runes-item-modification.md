# PoE2 0.5.0 Crafting, Runes, and Item Modification

Source: [Content Update 0.5.0 captured patch notes](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md)

## Runes of Aldur Crafting Core

- Remnants let players craft items by engraving Runic Recipes into Remnant slots, then defeating waves of enemies to claim the item. More runeshapes mean more waves and more runic enemy modifiers. [L42-L45](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L42-L45)
- Remnant monsters can drop Verisium, and Farrow's campaign quests unlock new crafting features. [L46-L48](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L46-L48)
- Verisium Runeforging adds Runic Ward to armour, with no downside below level 55 and defence tradeoffs above that. [L49](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L49)
- 13 Alloy currencies replace existing modifiers with specific crafted modifiers, similar to Perfect Essences. [L51](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L51)
- Unique Verisium Runeforging upgrades low-level unique weapon and armour base types and can add Runic Ward to higher-level unique armour. Kalguuran uniques have additional interactions. [L52-L53](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L52-L53), [L990](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L990)
- New rune categories include Ancient weapon-type runes, Mythical early-game runes, Fluxes for resistance conversion, Meta crafting runes, unique-destroying property runes, and Runic Ward runes. [L54-L59](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L54-L59)
- Kalguuran craftable rewards now list 23 Kalguuran Skills and 7 Kalguuran Supports from Remnants; the update log explicitly adds Rain of Blades and Wardbound Minions to the skill list and removes Kalguuran Conviction from the support list. [L60-L61](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L60-L61), [L892](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L892), [L936](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L936)

## League Mechanic Crafting Systems

- Delirium's Liquid Emotions can craft additional jewel mods, while Potent emotions can craft powerful jewel modifiers and instill new notables. [L119](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L119), [L123](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L123)
- Ancient Emotions can craft Timelost Jewels, and Ancient Potent emotions can craft powerful Timelost Jewel modifiers outside the normal mod pool. [L126](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L126)
- Breach's Genesis Tree consumes Wombgifts and Hiveblood to craft rings, amulets, belts, and currency. Its nodes affect crafted outputs. [L136-L138](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L136-L138)
- Genesis Tree adds exclusive ring, amulet, and belt bases, plus Caster and Minion mods for rings and belts. [L140-L143](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L140-L143)
- Catalysts no longer drop from monsters and are now Genesis Tree sourced; 12 new Catalysts add new jewel quality modifiers. [L144-L145](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L144-L145)
- Fate of the Vaal now has Vaal Armourer's, Blacksmith's, Arcanist's, and Catalysing Infusers, each tied to item classes and requiring at least 20% quality. [L187-L190](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L187-L190)
- Vaal Cultivation Orb outcomes on uniques were updated, including Atziri's Rule, Atziri's Splendour, The Covenant, Hateforge, Rathpith Globe, Shackles of the Wretched, and The Vertex. [L192-L199](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L192-L199)

## Currency, Corruption, Omens, and Deprecated Systems

- Greater and Perfect currencies are rarer, while Transmutation and Augmentation are much rarer. Greater Transmutation and Augmentation now have lower minimum modifier levels and can drop in Act 4. [L590-L591](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L590-L591)
- Divine Orbs are more common. [L592](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L592)
- Corruption and sanctification outcomes now multiply existing modifier values instead of randomizing each value independently. [L593-L594](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L593-L594)
- Omen of Corruption can no longer be obtained. [L595](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L595)
- Essences are more common in the campaign, but Essences of the Infinite make up a smaller share. [L597](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L597)
- Recombinator and Omen of Recombination are disabled, with existing Omens of Recombination deleted on login. [L212](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L212)
- Expedition vendors can now use stash currency for purchases; old Expedition artifacts and related items were removed or restricted on the Currency Exchange, legacy Expedition currency can be vendored for Gold, and deprecated Expedition/Recombinator inventories can be reclaimed with `/reclaimexpeditioninventories`. [L215-L220](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L215-L220)
- Exile's Pilfering Ring and Tracking Scroll of Crafting now track Hinekora's Lock and Fracturing Orb. [L737](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L737)

## Base Item and Modifier Retuning

- Martial Weapons and Flasks found during the campaign are more likely to be the highest available base type. [L596](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L596)
- Armour and Evasion values from items and modifiers increase at higher levels, while Energy Shield is only higher around level 65 and unchanged at 80+. [L598-L600](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L598-L600)
- Existing base Armour adjusts automatically; Armour modifiers can be divined. [L602](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L602)
- Warden and Guardian Bow implicits, Rabid/Fury/Maji Talisman implicits, and Voltfang/Thunder Talisman implicits were adjusted. [L605-L607](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L605-L607)
- Alternate Quality items from Breach Catalysts can be salvaged for partial Catalyst return. [L608](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L608)
- Skill level modifiers on weapons and quivers were lowered or gated to higher levels, and Perfect Essence of Battle now grants fewer attack skill levels. [L609-L616](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L609-L616)
- New Surpassing chance to fire an additional Arrow modifiers were added for Quivers. [L615](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L615)
- Jeweller's Orbs can now be used on items with granted skills to increase sockets on those granted skills; items with multiple granted skills update all of them. [L588](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L588)
- Crafted modifiers are now guaranteed, but items are limited to one crafted modifier; Desecrated modifiers no longer count as crafted, but items are limited to one Desecrated modifier. [L589](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L589)
- Several low-level weapon accuracy and hybrid modifiers now have higher minimum levels. [L671-L674](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L671-L674)

## Rune, Idol, Soul Core, and Jewel Changes

- Body, Mind, Rebirth, Inspiration, Stone, Vision, resistance, attribute, and Standard Rune bonded modifier values were broadly retuned. [L618-L630](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L618-L630)
- Rebirth Rune Energy Shield Recharge values on Wands and Staves are heavily reduced, and Craiceann's Rune of Recovery is reduced on body armour. [L631-L634](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L631-L634)
- Seasonal runes from Lady Hestra, Thane Leld, and Thane Myrk now add damage conditionally against chilled/frozen, shocked, or ignited enemies. [L635-L637](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L635-L637)
- All Idols can now socket into Sceptres, and many Idol socket and bonded modifiers were updated. [L638-L656](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L638-L656)
- Atmohua's Soul Core of Retreat now grants ailment and stun threshold from maximum Energy Shield instead of faster Energy Shield recharge start. [L657](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L657)
- Quipolatl's Thesis was retuned across Helmet, Glove, and Body Armour socket outcomes, while Guatelitzi's Thesis now grants Armour equal to 35% of Life lost from hits in the past 8 seconds from helmets. [L963-L964](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L963-L964)
- Ruby and Sapphire Jewel modifier ranges were adjusted. [L658-L659](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L658-L659)

## Energy Shield and Other Affix Pool Changes

- Faster-start Energy Shield Recharge suffixes can no longer roll on Foci, Intelligence Body Armours, or Jewels, while Energy Shield Recharge Rate suffixes can roll on Intelligence Body Armours. [L660-L661](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L660-L661)
- Arcane Raiment and Sacramental Robe implicits now grant Energy Shield Recharge Rate instead of faster recharge start. [L662](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L662)
- Fevered can no longer roll on Time-Lost Sapphire and Sapphire Jewels. [L663](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L663)
- Diffusion, Dispersal, and Enlivening suffixes can no longer roll on body armour and foci; Buffering, Ardour, and Suffusion values are reduced. [L664-L667](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L664-L667)
- Ring and amulet Cast Speed modifiers are no longer shared with Foci and Wands, and their highest tiers now roll at specific higher levels. [L668](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L668)
- Jewel Banner Area of Effect modifiers have lower values. [L669](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L669)
- Desecrated Instant Leech modifiers can no longer roll, though existing items are unaffected. [L670](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L670)
- Physical Attack Damage leeched as Life/Mana modifiers on Gloves and Rings now roll at lower levels with first tiers removed, Parasite/Thirsty suffixes can no longer roll, remaining Life/Mana leech suffix tiers have lower level requirements, and Vampire/Desperate can roll on Bows and Crossbows. [L965-L975](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L965-L975)

## Crafting-Relevant Bug Fixes

- Corrupting Essences no longer sometimes remove the Essence. [L832](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L832)
- Essence monster minions now correctly receive Rare-pack stat increases. [L833](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L833)
- Items without modifiers no longer appear in lower-level Ritual encounters. [L835](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L835)
- Soul Core Ultimatum encounters can no longer be completed using a single Soul Core. [L843](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L843)

## Practical Read

- Crafting in 0.5.0 shifts strongly toward league-specific deterministic systems: Remnants/Runes for broad item generation, Genesis Tree for jewelry and Catalysts, and Fate of the Vaal for item-class-specific Vaal modification.
- The removal of Recombinator and Omen of Recombination matters for high-end item synthesis; new rune and essence systems are the likely replacement planning axis.
- Atlas specialization now gates several crafting-relevant drops, so crafting plans need an endgame farming route, not just a bench or currency plan.
