# PoE2 0.5.0 Uniques and Build-Enabling Items

Source: [Content Update 0.5.0 captured patch notes](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md)

## New Unique Item Pool

- The patch adds a large set of new uniques, including Berek's Grip, Berek's Pass, Berek's Respite, Brutus' Lead Sprinkler, Cat O' Nine Tails, Facebreaker, Loreweave, Mageblood, Split Personality, Voices, and many more. [L298-L339](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L298-L339)
- The important knowledge-base implication is that several PoE1-defining names enter PoE2's item pool, so build archetypes around unarmed scaling, flask power, jewel sockets, and resistance/item-stat conversion may need fresh PoE2-specific notes.

## Unique Upgrade and Vaal Outcome Changes

- Unique Verisium Runeforging can upgrade low-level unique weapons and armour, improving weapon damage or armour defences and adding Runic Ward to armour. [L52](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L52), [L840](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L840)
- Kalguuran uniques have additional Unique Verisium Runeforging properties. [L53](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L53)
- Vaal Cultivation Orb outcomes changed for Atziri's Rule, Atziri's Splendour, The Covenant, Hateforge, Rathpith Globe, Shackles of the Wretched, and The Vertex. [L192-L199](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L192-L199)

## Defensive and Resource Build Items

- Ab Aeterno loses its old dodge-roll speed penalty scaling and gains much higher Armour, Evasion, and Energy Shield plus slow immunity while sprinting and Overencumbrance on dodge roll. [L525](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L525)
- Apep's Supremacy loses faster Energy Shield recharge start. [L526](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L526)
- Atziri's Acuity is reworked around Armour, maximum Life, Physical Attack Damage leeched as Life, Blood Loss, Vaal Pact, and Herald of the Royal Queen. [L527](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L527)
- Sierran Inheritance now gives Energy Shield Recharge Rate rather than faster recharge start. [L528](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L528)
- Brass Dome has lower increased Armour. [L531](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L531)
- Keeper of the Arc has higher Armour and Energy Shield values. [L541](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L541)
- Sine Aequo has higher Armour/Evasion/Energy Shield and changes from an immobilized-enemy more-damage stat to increased damage against immobilized enemies. [L550](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L550)
- Svalinn's Cast on Block now makes supported skills cost nothing and gains Runic Ward. [L552](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L552)

## Damage and Archetype-Defining Items

- Blackflame is reworked into Withered increasing Fire damage taken, chaos-damage Ignites, non-expiring Withered on ignited enemies, and stronger Ignite magnitude with shorter duration. [L529](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L529)
- Blistering Bond now enables Fire Damage to contribute to Bleeding Magnitude and makes Bleeding deal Fire damage. [L530](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L530)
- Carnage Heart now gives increased damage while leeching. [L532](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L532)
- Death's Harp trades flat additional arrows for high Surpassing chance to fire an additional arrow. [L534](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L534)
- Eshtera's Path, Safrin's Resolve, and Zaida's Longevity are converted away from area-penalty resistance immunity and into resistance scaling based on equipped-item resistance modifiers. [L536-L537](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L536-L537), [L547](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L547), [L553](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L553)
- Hyrri's Ire has lower Evasion and lower extra Cold Damage. [L540](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L540)
- Levinstone loses +1 Lightning Skills and instead gives Lightning Skills an extra chain. [L542](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L542)
- Plaguefinger now prevents Elemental Ailments. [L544](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L544)
- Prized Pain now gives a chance to deal Thorns damage on melee hit rather than only to stunned enemies. [L545](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L545)
- Radiant Grief doubles the base Fire Damage for enemies ignited in your presence. [L546](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L546)
- Seed of Cataclysm changes from flat Critical Spell Damage Bonus to reduced bonus per recent spell crit plus Lucky spell critical damage chance. [L548](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L548)

## Skill, Minion, Totem, and Flask Items

- Chober Chaber now grants +2-3 to all Minion Skills. [L533](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L533)
- Glowswarm grants Guard from Mana Flask recovery. [L537](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L537)
- Hollow Mask grants Wildwood's Gifts, shares Remnant effects with allies in your presence, and improves Remnant Skill reservation efficiency. [L538](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L538)
- Reverie replaces Husk of Dreams and becomes a Life Flask engine with constant non-unique Life Flask effects, inability to use Life Flasks, non-instant recovery, and less recovery. [L539](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L539)
- Soul Mantle loses reduced Totem Life and gains +75 Spirit. [L551](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L551)
- Zerphi's Genesis converts consumed Life Flask charges into Charm charges and gives corrupted-gem skill cost efficiency during flask effects. [L554](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L554)

## Item Pool and Storage Notes

- Megalomaniac now drops identified. [L543](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L543)
- Sekhema's Resolve rings were renamed into Safrin's Resolve, Zaida's Longevity, and Eshtera's Path. [L549](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L549)
- Upgraded variants of Grip of Kulemak can now be stored in the Unique Stash Tab. [L807](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L807)

## Practical Read

- The biggest build-watch items are Facebreaker, Mageblood, Loreweave, Voices, Split Personality, Svalinn, Atziri's Acuity, Blackflame, Death's Harp, and the renamed resistance-scaling Sekhema rings.
- Several changed uniques now lean harder into PoE2 mechanics: Runic Ward, Blood Loss, Presence, Remnants, Spirit, Surpassing chance, and flask/Charm charge loops.
- Existing-item notes matter: some changes are divinable or affect existing items, while others explicitly do not. Each item page should track this separately.
