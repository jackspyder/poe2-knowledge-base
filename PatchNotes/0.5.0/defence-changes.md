# PoE2 0.5.0 Defence Changes

Source: [Content Update 0.5.0 captured patch notes](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md)

This is an extracted working view of defence-related entries. The source capture is unchanged; links point back to the relevant source lines.

## Runic Ward and General Defence Rules

- Verisium Runeforging can add Runic Ward to armour. Lower-level armour gains it without a downside; higher-level armour trades regular base defences for Ward. [L49](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L49)
- Runic Ward is a new defence that takes over at 1 life and regenerates separately from life. [L50](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L50)
- Unique Verisium Runeforging can upgrade low-level unique armour bases and add Runic Ward; higher-level unique armour can also trade regular defences for Ward. [L52](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L52), [L990](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L990)
- 15+ Runic Ward runes were added. [L59](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L59)
- Maximum Runic Ward now contributes to starting Honour in Trial of the Sekhemas. [L271](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L271)
- The "Defences" keyword now explicitly means Armour, Evasion, and Energy Shield. It does not include Runic Ward, resistances, block, or other protection. [L272](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L272)
- Trusted Kinship no longer applies a player defence penalty or grants companions defence based on the player's defences. [L406](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L406)
- Svalinn gains +50-100 Runic Ward and changes Cast on Block to make supported skills cost nothing. [L583](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L583)
