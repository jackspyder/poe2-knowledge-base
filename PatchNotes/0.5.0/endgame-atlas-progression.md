# PoE2 0.5.0 Endgame and Atlas Progression

Source: [Content Update 0.5.0 captured patch notes](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md)

## Core Atlas Direction

- The Atlas now has fixed points of interest, making progression objective-driven rather than mostly exploratory. [L77](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L77)
- League mechanics on the Atlas now have questlines that introduce the mechanic and take you through its stages up to the Pinnacle Boss. [L78](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L78)
- Pinnacle Bosses now have deterministic quest versions in addition to repeatable higher-challenge farm versions. [L79](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L79), [L227](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L227)
- Existing Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary Calamity Fragments were converted to Ancient, Faded, and Weathered Crisis Fragments respectively. [L941-L943](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L941-L943)
- 30 new endgame map areas were added. [L80](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L80)
- Atlas completion at fixed locations is shared with party members. [L253](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L253)

## Origins of Divinity and Atlas Tree Progression

- The main endgame storyline now centers on the Fortress, Gates, and Precursor weapons. [L83](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L83)
- Completing the first tower raises the Fortress. Maps inside it grant Atlas Passive Tree points, replacing the previous Atlas point acquisition model. [L84](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L84), [L85](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L85)
- The Atlas Tree has more than 300 nodes, and Fortress completion provides enough points to fully allocate it. Multi-choice nodes can be swapped freely. [L86](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L86), [L87](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L87)
- Fortress maps can contain Ancient Modifiers, including modifiers that add secondary content and implicitly add that content to the map. [L88](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L88)
- 40+ Ancient Modifiers can appear outside the Fortress. [L89](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L89)
- Gateway maps, new Citadels, Enigma chambers, Arbiter of Ash relocation, and Arbiter of Divinity define the new Pinnacle access chain. [L90-L95](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L90-L95)
- Killing Arbiter of Divinity five times can alternatively complete Fortress sections for Atlas Tree points. [L96](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L96)
- Existing Atlas progression is reset, but tablets and waystones continue to work; Atlas Tree points require the Origins of Divinity storyline. [L223](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L223)

## Masters of the Atlas

- Masters of the Atlas adds an endgame progression layer with swappable master alignments such as Doryani, Hilda, and Jado. [L99](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L99)
- Each master has 12 nodes, with 4 active at a time, and selections can be changed freely. [L100](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L100)
- All three masters can be allocated simultaneously, with a quick select before running a map. [L101](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L101)
- Master rows unlock by completing master missions. Doryani starts from corruption nexus, Hilda from Hilda's Campsite, and Jado from an anomaly map. [L102-L105](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L102-L105)

## League Mechanic Hubs and Pinnacle Paths

- Runes of Aldur has a hub at The Ruins of Kingsmarch, an ocean exploration replacement for Logbooks, faction leader island encounters, and a key path from Olroth to its Pinnacle Boss. [L64-L69](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L64-L69)
- Expedition's Standard migration removes old artifact currencies from the Currency Exchange, moves some Omens to Standard-only exchange listings, converts legacy Expedition currency to vendor Gold, converts existing Expedition vendor hideout NPCs to doodads, and adds `/reclaimexpeditioninventories` for deprecated inventories. [L216-L220](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L216-L220)
- Delirium has The Withered Willow hub, a revamped Delirium Atlas Tree, Grand Mirrors, Trial of Madness, scalable fog up to 200% delirious, and Simulacrum access into the new Delirium Pinnacle Boss. [L109-L126](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L109-L126)
- Breach has The Monastery of the Keepers hub, a revamped Breach Atlas Tree, Atlas-spawned Breach Domains, Breach Hives, Sky Hives, Sky Fortresses, and Tul/Esh keys to Breach Pinnacle access. [L131-L152](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L131-L152)
- Ritual has Caer Tarth, a revamped Ritual Atlas Tree, Rite of the Nameless five-map chain, and Queen in the Mists access through a specific Atlas node. [L156-L165](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L156-L165)
- Fate of the Vaal has its own Atlas Tree, Lira Vaal temple region, Temple Precursor Tablets, Tier 4 room upgrades, and more explicit room planning. [L170-L175](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L170-L175)
- Abyss has large Atlas cracks that close into Abyssal Depths boss fights, plus a revamped Abyss Atlas Tree. [L202-L203](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L202-L203)

## Waystones, Tablets, and Map Modifiers

- Empty tablet slots now increase random non-tablet league content, so fully slotted maps emphasize chosen tablet content. [L229](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L229)
- Waystones must be identified before map-device activation. [L230](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L230)
- Some content and items require Atlas specialization, including Exceptional Items, Fracturing Orbs, Basic tablets, and Overseer precursor tablets. [L232](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L232)
- Unforeseen Consequences no longer adds 4-6 additional Rare Monsters from Abysses in maps; existing versions can be made worse with a Divine Orb. [L235](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L235)
- Base map spawn chances for Essences, Azmeri Spirits, Shrines, Strongboxes, Summoning Circles, and Rogue Exiles were lowered because Atlas Tree scaling is larger. [L236](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L236)
- General Precursor Tablet modifiers were retuned closer to Waystone modifier bonuses, with reduced roll variance. [L238](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L238)
- Several Waystone modifiers can no longer roll, and many map modifiers were reassigned between prefixes and suffixes based on whether they affect monster output or player/monster defences. [L239-L242](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L239-L242)
- Waystone modifiers adding monster damage, elemental resistance penetration, or reduced player maximum resistances were reduced across the board, and the Extra Projectiles to Monsters Waystone modifier was disabled. [L944-L945](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L944-L945)
- Waystone bonuses now provide multiplicative Monster Effectiveness, Pack Size, Item Rarity, Monster Rarity, or Waystone Drop Chance. [L244](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L244)
- Pack Size now gives a chance for an additional Rare Monster in rare packs, and Monster Rarity is a new stat affecting magic/rare monster chances and rare modifier counts. [L245](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L245), [L246](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L246)
- Chaotic waystone Omens now prevent certain modifier types rather than guaranteeing them, and Omen of Chaotic Effectiveness can exclude Monster Effectiveness modifiers. [L249-L251](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L249-L251)
- Shrine base duration is now 45 seconds, Strongboxes can no longer roll "Contains Identified Items", and nonfunctional tablet modifiers such as extra Breach clasped hands or extra Delirium rewards are disabled. [L255-L257](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L255-L257)
- Many Act 4 bosses can now appear in the Stone Circle map mechanic. [L258](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L258)

## UI and Bug Fixes

- Atlas Map search, further zoom-out, clickable Atlas town travel, larger content indicators, and new Waystone art improve navigation and scanning. [L224-L226](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L224-L226), [L254-L259](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L254-L259)
- Abyss Precursor Tablets no longer incorrectly add one extra Abyss each. [L804](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L804)
- Trial of the Sekhemas Hourglass trials had a monster activation bug fixed. [L984](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L984)
- Boss health bars no longer show inside the Atlas screen. [L857](../poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L857)

## Practical Read

- Atlas planning moves from "choose scarce points" to "eventually own the full tree, then choose map-level specialization through master selection, tablets, waystones, and league hubs."
- Early endgame priority likely becomes reaching fixed Atlas objectives and Fortress progression, because that gates full Atlas Tree allocation.
- Farming strategies should separate quest-version boss progression from infinite-farm versions; the deterministic quest path is for unlocks, while the farm version is the repeat loop.
