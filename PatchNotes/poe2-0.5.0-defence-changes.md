# PoE2 0.5.0 Defence Changes

Source: [Content Update 0.5.0 captured patch notes](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md)

This is an extracted working view of defence-related entries. The source capture is unchanged; links point back to the relevant source lines.

## Runic Ward and General Defence Rules

- Verisium Runeforging can add Runic Ward to armour. Lower-level armour gains it without a downside; higher-level armour trades regular base defences for Ward. [L49](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L49)
- Runic Ward is a new defence that takes over at 1 life and regenerates separately from life. [L50](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L50)
- Unique Verisium Runeforging can upgrade low-level unique armour bases and add Runic Ward; higher-level unique armour can also trade regular defences for Ward. [L52](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L52), [L840](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L840)
- 15+ Runic Ward runes were added. [L59](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L59)
- Maximum Runic Ward now contributes to starting Honour in Trial of the Sekhemas. [L260](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L260)
- The "Defences" keyword now explicitly means Armour, Evasion, and Energy Shield. It does not include Runic Ward, resistances, block, or other protection. [L261](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L261)
- Trusted Kinship no longer applies a player defence penalty or grants companions defence based on the player's defences. [L387](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L387)
- Svalinn gains +50-100 Runic Ward and changes Cast on Block to make supported skills cost nothing. [L552](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L552)

## Energy Shield

- Energy Shield leech now follows the one-active-instance rule, and high damage hits are capped for leech calculation. [L262](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L262), [L263](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L263)
- Atziri's Splendour's Vaal Cultivation outcome for maximum Energy Shield is reduced to +66-100 from +100-200. [L194](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L194)
- Fortified Aegis replaces Adamant Recovery and grants 100% increased Armour, Evasion, and Energy Shield from equipped shield. [L392](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L392)
- Arcane Mixtures loses Energy Shield Recharge Rate and now grants cast speed after using a Mana Flask. [L393](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L393)
- Bastion of the Forest, Dependable Ward, Devoted Protector, Mystic Stance, and Quick Response all have lower faster-start-of-Energy-Shield-Recharge values. - Chakra of Breathing now gives flat faster recharge start instead of scaling recharge rate from Dexterity. [L396](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L396)
- Convalescence now has lower faster recharge start and lower reduced recharge rate. [L398](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L398)
- Core of the Guardian now grants block chance with 20% reduced maximum Energy Shield, replacing shield-based Armour/Evasion/Energy Shield scaling. [L399](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L399)
- Covering Ward now gives 25 Energy Shield on block and block chance, but loses Energy Shield Recharge Rate. [L400](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L400)
- Energising Archon, Energising Deflection, Essence Infusion, Immortal Infamy, and Refocus all move away from Energy Shield Recharge Rate into other stats or faster recharge start. [L409](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L409), [L410](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L410), [L411](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L411), [L415](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L415), [L421](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L421)
- Patient Barrier is reduced to 50% increased maximum Energy Shield from 60%. [L418](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L418)
- Rapid Recharge now grants 12% faster recharge start and 12% recharge rate, down from 25% each. [L420](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L420)
- Shimmering now gives faster recharge start and Evasion after being hit, losing its not-hit-recently Energy Shield recovery rate. [L423](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L423)

- Ab Aeterno and Sine Aequo have higher Armour/Evasion/Energy Shield values. [L525](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L525), [L550](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L550)

- Sierran Inheritance now grants Energy Shield Recharge Rate instead of faster recharge start. [L528](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L528)

- Faster-start Energy Shield Recharge suffixes can no longer roll on Foci, Intelligence Body Armours, or Jewels; Energy Shield Recharge Rate suffixes can roll on Intelligence Body Armours. [L627](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L627), [L628](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L628)

- Bug fixes: skills costing a percentage of maximum Energy Shield can be used with zero maximum Energy Shield, and the depleted Energy Shield audio cue now plays for damage over time. [L729](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L729), [L801](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L801)

- Obsessive Rituals now applies 50% less Armour and Evasion Rating, up from 35% less. [L380](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L380)
- Fortified Aegis grants 100% increased Armour, Evasion, and Energy Shield from equipped shield. [L392](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L392)
- The Quick Fox replacement path adds 20% increased Evasion Rating while moving to the preceding small attribute passives. [L403](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L403)
- Shimmering now grants 20% increased Evasion if hit recently. [L423](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L423)
- Ghost Dance now regenerates Energy Shield equal to 2% of Evasion Rating per second after losing a Ghost Shroud recently. [L459](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L459), [L841](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L841)
- Magma Barrier shield scaling is reduced to 5-7 added Fire Damage per 15 Armour and Evasion. [L465](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L465)
- Refraction I and II make supported Banner skills grant Deflection Rating based on allies' Evasion Rating. [L516](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L516), [L517](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L517)
- Ab Aeterno and Sine Aequo have higher Armour/Evasion/Energy Shield values. [L525](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L525), [L550](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L550)
- Hyrri's Ire has lower Evasion Rating, reduced from 200-250% to 100-150%. [L540](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L540)

- Idol of Ralakesh can grant 40% increased Armour, Evasion, and Energy Shield while your companion is in your presence. [L610](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L610)
- Bug fix: The Bloated Miller's backhand swing can now be blocked, parried, or evaded. [L758](./poe2-0.5.0-return-of-the-ancients.md#L758)
