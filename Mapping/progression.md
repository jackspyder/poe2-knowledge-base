# POE2 T1-T15 Progression Strategy (League Start / Expert)

## 1. THE ARBITER RUSH (T1 to T15)
The goal is to unlock the **Arbiter of Ash** and T15 sustain as fast as possible.

### Progression Tiers & Rolling Strategy
| Tier | Crafting Strategy | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T1 - T7** | **Transmutation only**. Save Alchs for high-tier bases. | Reveal Towers + Move towards Citadels. |
| **T8 - T12** | **Transmutation + Augmentation**. Aim for 3+ mods. | Establish Citadel anchors. |
| **T13 - T15** | **Orb of Alchemy**. Mandatory for T15 sustain. | Unlock Arbiter of Ash & Nexus Passive Points. |

**Expert Tip**: Doryani (NPC) sells maps 1 tier lower than your highest completion. Use this to fill gaps instead of trading early.

---

## 2. TOWER CHASING (Atlas Reveal Meta)
In **v0.4**, Towers are the primary engine for Atlas progression.

- **The Reveal**: Clearing a Tower reveals a large radius of the Atlas. Prioritize Towers over map completion early.
- **Precursor Rewards**: Every Tower rewards 1 **Precursor Tablet**.
- **Speed Clear**: Do NOT full clear. Head straight to the **Precursor Beacon** to unlock the area and get the tablet.
- **Waystone Scaling**: Use your lowest tier Waystones for Tower runs to save high-tier resources for boss completion.

---

## 3. EARLY EXPERIENCE MAXIMIZATION
Before you reach "Juicing" stages, use these low-cost methods to scale character level.

### High-Density Exp Targets
- **Abyss Tablets**: Highest base monster density for low cost. Prioritize **Extra Pits**.
- **Delirium Tablets**: Best for flat exp scaling via "Distilled Emotions" (Liquid Emotions in v0.4).
- **Reset Mechanics**: Use `CTRL + Click` to reset high-density zones like **Hunting Grounds** or **Dreadnought** if you are under-leveled for your current Waystone tier.

---

## 4. THE ATLAS PASSIVE RUSH (40 Points Total)
Experts prioritize passive points over map tier early, as they provide the character power needed to sustain T15+ juicing.

### Nexus of Corruption (30 Points)
Earn **5 points** per tier jump. You must use a Waystone with **at least 4 modifiers** (hold Alt to count).
- **Tiers**: 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15.
- **Goal**: Clear the map -> Defeat Boss/Rare -> Defeat **Corrupted Boss** -> Use **Crystalline Core of Knowledge**.
- **Visuals**: Look for **Red Tendrils** or **Black Miasma** at the edge of the Atlas fog.

### Unique Map Completion (10 Points)
Earn **2 points** per unique map (up to 5 different maps).
- **Icons**: Look for the **Orange Map Icons** on the Atlas.
- **Tip**: Buying unique maps early from players or Doryani is the fastest way to hit your first 20-30 points.
