# POE2 T15+ Breach Strategy (High-End / Expert)

## 1. TABLET SETUP (Splinter Maxing)
High-end Breach farming focuses on **Splinter Density** and **Hand Spawns**. Requires 3 tablet slots (6-mod T15 maps).

### Priority Tablet Modifiers (0.3.1+)
- **Additional Hands**: +3 Clasped Hands per Breach (Absolute priority).
- **Splinter Quantity**: +75% Increased Quantity of Splinters dropped by monsters.
- **Additional Breaches**: Chance for map to contain an additional Breach.
- **Monster Density (Rising Pyre)**: 25% Increased Monster density within Breaches.

**Expert Tip**: Target "Hand" nodes over general density. Each Hand has a high base splinter drop; scaling their quantity via tablets is the primary profit lever.

## 2. ATLAS PASSIVE SETUPS

### The "Full Juice" Configuration
- **Frantic Invasion**: Maximize Breach monster counts.
- **Crumbling Walls**: +1 Breach per map.
- **Waking Nightmare**: 60% chance for double splinters (Mandatory, necessitates high defensive layers).
- **Zash Madness**: 50% effectiveness to Rare Breach monsters (Scales item quality/quantity).
- **Sustained Siege**: 30% slower opening/closing (Required for full clear in large/complex layouts).

## 4. PROFIT & EXECUTION (T15+)

### The Splinter Loop
- **Cost**: ~3 Divine Orbs (Tablets + High-end Waystones).
- **Yield**: 600–800 Splinters per map (depending on Hand rolls).
- **Primary Targets**: Flawless Breachstones and unique Boss drops from **Xesht**.

### Bossing: Xesht, the Chosen
- **Arena**: Extremely tight.
- **Shadow Chase**: Lethal. Never stop moving.
- **Drops**: Guaranteed Uniques and Atlas Skill Books (on first kill/completion).
