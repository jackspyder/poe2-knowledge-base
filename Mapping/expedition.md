# POE2 T15+ Expedition Strategy (Expert)

## 1. TABLET SETUP (Logbook Hunting)
High-end Expedition is defined by **Logbook Velocity** and **Runic Monster Duplication**.

**v0.4 Reward Buff**: Artifact drop rates doubled; Exotic Coinage increased by 50%.

### Unique Tablet: Forgotten by Time
- **Implicit**: Applies Expedition to 12-18 maps. (No tower required in v0.4).
- **Explicit 1**: Monsters spawn at 50% life (Speed clear).
- **Explicit 2**: **Runic Monsters are duplicated** (Absolute priority for high-end).

### Standard Tablet Modifiers
- **Logbook Quantity**: 10%+ Increased quantity of Logbooks from Runic Monsters.
- **Rare Markers**: +X Runic Monster Markers per encounter.
- **Artifact Quantity**: For sustaining vendor refreshes (Tujen/Dannig equivalents).

---

## 2. ATLAS PASSIVE SETUPS

### The "Detailed Records" Core
- **Detailed Records**: 30% Logbook drop quantity + Guaranteed 3 implicit modifiers.
- **Disturbed Rest**: Maximizes Runic Monster markers per map.
- **Extreme Archaeology**: One giant explosive. (Expert only: Requires high-damage/defensive coverage but saves 30-40s per map).
- **Legendary Battles**: Increases Rare monster count and Exotic Coinage (Vendor currency).

---

## 3. T15+ LOGBOOK OPTIMIZATION

| Priority Target | Reason | Expert Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lush Isle** | Currency/Raw Exalts. | Focus on **Arenui Quartz** nodes. |
| **Tujen Equivalent** | Stackable currency income. | Bulk buy artifacts; ignore cheap items. |
| **Dannig Equivalent** | Reroll/Logbook sustain. | Buy all Logbooks and reroll currency. |

**T15 Waystone Requirement**: T15 maps drop Level 79 Logbooks. Use **Corrupted** or **Irradiated** modifiers to push to Level 81-83 Logbooks for elite reward brackets.

---

## 4. EXECUTION (THE RED FLAG)
- **Primary Target**: Always prioritize **Red Flagpoles** (Runic Monsters).
- **Remnant Priority**: Quant/Rarity > Artifacts > Monster buffs.
- **Safety**: Avoid "Immune to [Your Damage Type]" or "100% block" unless your build ignores them.
