# POE2 T15+ Abyss Strategy (End-Game / Expert)

## 1. TABLET SETUP (Density & Spire Fishing)
High-end Abyss focuses on forcing **Stygian Spires** (replacing Troves) and maximizing **Desecrated Currency** drops. Requires 3 tablet slots (6-mod T15 maps).

### Priority Tablet Modifiers (v0.4+)
- **Extra Pits**: +7-9 Additional Pits (Mandatory for high-end density).
- **Lichborn Chance**: Increased chance for Abyssal Depths/Bosses.
- **Monster Density**: 25-30% Increased Abyssal Monster density.
- **Magic Monsters**: 25% Chance for pits to spawn all Magic monsters.

**Expert Tip**: Target "Extra Pits" suffix first. Each pit is a multiplier for **Desecrated Currency** and unique jewel drops.

---

## 2. ATLAS PASSIVE SETUPS

### The "Lich Hunter" Configuration
- **Abyssal Ire**: 100% chance for Abyss to lead to a Boss (Essential for T15+ unique item velocity).
- **Lightless Legions**: +25% Monsters + Amanamu loyalty (Highest value mob type).
- **Close to the Surface**: Waystone modifiers apply to Abyssal Depths (Critical for T15+ juicing).
- **Blackblooded Dominance**: 25% increased chance for **Desecrated Currency** (The primary high-end profit metric).
- **Dark Bloodlines**: 25% chance for all spawns to be Magic rarity (Huge XP/Loot scaling).

## 4. PROFIT & EXECUTION (T15+)

### High-Value Targets
- **Stygian Vise**: Guaranteed drop from **Stygian Spires** (T15+ ilvl 82+ bases are high-demand).
- **Desecrated Currency**: New high-tier currency used for Waystone modification.
- **Darkness Enthroned**: Target unique drop from Liches.
- **Ghastly Eye Jewels**: Check meta for Minion-stacker demand (Highest liquidity jewel type).

### Execution Strategy
- **Overrun Events**: Do not burst the Spire immediately. Allow it to spawn waves to maximize Desecrated Currency drops.
- **Depth Focus**: If a fissure leads to Abyssal Depths, take it. T15 Depths are Level 81-83 and scale uniquely with Waystone mods via **Close to the Surface**.
