# POE2 High-End Map Preparation (Elite Setup)

Expert-level tools for bypassing Atlas progression obstacles and setting up "Full Juice" farming environments. These tools are typically expensive early-league but mandatory for Week 2+ efficiency.

## 1. THE GRAND PROJECT (Grand Design Tablet)
The ultimate tool for Atlas manipulation.

| Feature | Effect | Expert Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Irradiation** | Irradiates all maps in a large radius. | Use to reveal high-density T15 clusters without clearing adjacent beacons/tethers. |
| **Bypass** | Grants access to *all* maps in range regardless of completion. | Use to "jump" into elite layouts (e.g., Willow/Hive) if your natural progression path is blocked. |
| **Juice Scaling** | Enhances the difficulty and reward floor of the region. | Mandatory for setting up "Permanent T15" farming regions. |

---

## 2. AN AUDIENCE WITH THE KING (Ritual Pinnacle)
The entry key for the **King in the Mists** boss encounter.

- **Source**: Primarily found via **Ritual Favours** (requires high tribute/deferral).
- **Goal**: Defeat the King for **8 total Ritual Passive Points** and high-value unique jewels (**Voices/Megalomaniac**).
- **Expert Tip**: Do not "buy" this early unless you can comfortably clear the fight; failure wastes the invitation and resets your tribute investment.

---

## 3. RELIQUARY KEYS (Azmeri & Vaal)
Access to the Vaults of Foil Uniques.

| Key Type | Reward Focus | Expert Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Azmeri Key** | Foiled High-End Uniques (Current League). | High Liquidity (~30 Div). Best to sell unless gambling for a Mirror-tier foil. |
| **Vaal Key** | Corrupted/Double-Corrupted High-End Uniques. | Extreme variance. Only for "Infinite Budget" stage. |

---

## 4. LIQUID EMOTIONS (Waystone/Amulet Juicing)
Refined from **Distilled Emotions** via Delirium Bosses.

- **Waystone Scaling**: Use Tier 4 Liquid Emotions on T15 Waystones to force **Irradiated** or **Corrupted** status (pushing monster level to 83+).
- **Amulet Anointing**: Mandatory for end-game build-enabling passives (Reservation, Life/ES scaling).
- **Market Liquidity**: High. Sells instantly in bulk.
