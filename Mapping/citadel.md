# POE2 T15+ Citadel & Arbiter Strategy (Expert)

Citadel farming is the premier "Boss Rushing" strategy in v0.4, yielding 15-30 Div/hr.

## 1. THE CITADEL TRIAD
You must clear three specific Citadels to access the **Arbiter of Ash**.

| Citadel | Boss | Key Reward |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iron Citadel** | Count Geonor | Ancient Crisis Fragment |
| **Copper Citadel** | Jamanra | Faded Crisis Fragment |
| **Stone Citadel** | Doryani | Weathered Crisis Fragment |

**Fragment Strategy**: Each fragment sells for ~5-8 Div. Selling fragments is more consistent; clearing the Arbiter is a high-variance gamble for "Secret Flame" (~30 Div).

---

## 2. TABLET SYNERGIES
High-end Citadel farming uses the **Grand Project** tablet to reveal and jump between Citadels without clearing trash maps.

- **Overseer Tablet**: Mandatory. Adds +1 Map Level and guarantees boss drop quality.
- **Duplicate Bosses**: Synergize with Atlas nodes to spawn 2-3 bosses per Citadel for multiple fragment chances.

---

## 3. ATLAS PASSIVE SETUPS
- **Ancient Relics**: Increases fragment drop rates.
- **Witness to History**: Enhances loot from Arbiter of Ash.
- **Speed Meta**: Take all nodes that increase **Citadel Spawn Rate** (66% buff in v0.4).

---

## 4. PROFIT METRICS
- **Fragment Rushing**: 15-20 Div/hr (Selling Fragments).
- **Arbiter Farming**: 25-35 Div/hr (Requires BiS build to clear in <2 mins).

---
